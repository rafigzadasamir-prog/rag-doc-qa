Nimbus Cloud Storage: Platform Documentation

Overview

Nimbus Cloud Storage is a secure, scalable object storage platform designed for development teams that need reliable file storage without operating their own infrastructure. Launched in 2019, Nimbus now stores over 4 petabytes of data for more than 12,000 customers across 40 countries. The platform exposes a simple REST API and client SDKs for Python, JavaScript, Go, and Java, letting engineering teams integrate file upload, retrieval, and sharing into their applications within a single afternoon. Nimbus is built on a distributed architecture that replicates every object across three independent availability zones, so a single data center outage never results in data loss. Unlike traditional file servers, Nimbus separates storage from compute, which means customers only pay for the capacity and bandwidth they actually use rather than provisioning fixed-size volumes in advance. The engineering team behind Nimbus focuses heavily on predictable latency, and the median object retrieval time across all regions is under 80 milliseconds.

Pricing Plans

Nimbus offers three pricing tiers: Starter, Professional, and Enterprise. The Starter plan costs $9 per month and includes 100 GB of storage, 50 GB of monthly egress bandwidth, and access for up to 3 team members. The Professional plan costs $49 per month and includes 1 TB of storage, 500 GB of monthly egress bandwidth, unlimited team members, and priority email support. The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and adds dedicated account management, single sign-on, audit logging, and a contractual uptime guarantee of 99.95 percent. All plans bill storage in excess of the included quota at $0.02 per GB per month, and egress bandwidth beyond the quota at $0.08 per GB. Customers can switch between plans at any time from the billing dashboard, and Nimbus prorates the difference for the remainder of the billing cycle. Annual billing is available on the Professional and Enterprise plans at a 15 percent discount compared to paying monthly.

Data Security and Encryption

All data stored in Nimbus is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption, with a unique data key generated for every object and wrapped by a master key stored in a separate hardware security module. Data in transit is protected by TLS 1.3 on every API endpoint, and Nimbus rejects any connection attempt that negotiates an older protocol version. Customers on the Enterprise plan can supply their own encryption keys through the bring-your-own-key program, giving them the ability to revoke Nimbus's access to their data at any time by disabling the key. Access to stored objects is controlled through signed URLs that expire after a configurable time-to-live, as well as fine-grained bucket policies that can restrict access by IP range, API scope, or team role. Nimbus undergoes an independent SOC 2 Type II audit every year and publishes the resulting report to customers under NDA upon request.

API Rate Limits

The Nimbus REST API enforces rate limits to keep the platform fair and responsive for every customer. Starter plan accounts are limited to 100 requests per minute per API key, Professional plan accounts are limited to 1,000 requests per minute, and Enterprise accounts can negotiate a custom ceiling based on expected traffic. When a client exceeds its limit, the API returns an HTTP 429 response with a Retry-After header indicating how many seconds to wait before retrying. Bulk operations such as multi-file uploads or recursive directory listings count against the same quota as individual requests, so high-volume integrations should use the batch endpoints, which combine up to 500 operations into a single rate-limited call. Nimbus recommends that client libraries implement exponential backoff starting at 1 second when they receive a 429 response, and the official SDKs do this automatically.

Backup and Data Retention Policy

Nimbus automatically versions every object, keeping up to 30 days of prior versions available for recovery even if a file is overwritten or deleted. Deleted objects move to a recovery bin for 30 days before being permanently purged, and any team member with the restore permission can recover them from the dashboard within that window. Enterprise customers can extend version retention up to 365 days and can configure legal holds that prevent specific objects from being purged regardless of the normal retention schedule. Nimbus replicates all data to a geographically separate backup region on a rolling basis, with a recovery point objective of 15 minutes and a recovery time objective of 4 hours in the event of a full regional failure. Customers can also schedule their own export jobs to download a complete snapshot of a bucket to external storage for long-term archival outside the platform.

Customer Support SLAs

Support response times vary by plan. Starter plan customers receive email support with a target first response time of 2 business days. Professional plan customers receive priority email support with a target first response time of 4 business hours. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 phone and chat support with a guaranteed first response time of 30 minutes for critical severity incidents and 4 hours for all other severities. Every Enterprise contract includes a named technical account manager who reviews usage and reliability quarterly. If Nimbus fails to meet the response time guaranteed in an Enterprise contract, the customer is entitled to a service credit equal to 10 percent of that month's invoice for each missed severity-one response, up to a maximum credit of 50 percent in a single billing cycle.

Account Onboarding and Setup

Creating a Nimbus account takes about five minutes. New customers sign up with a work email, verify it through a confirmation link, and are immediately placed into a 14-day trial of the Professional plan with no credit card required. During onboarding, the setup wizard walks new teams through creating their first storage bucket, generating an API key, and installing the SDK for their preferred programming language. Enterprise customers who need single sign-on can connect Nimbus to any SAML 2.0 or OIDC identity provider, including Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace, through the security settings page. Teams migrating from another storage provider can use the built-in migration tool, which connects to Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage, or Azure Blob Storage and copies objects into Nimbus in the background while preserving existing folder structure and metadata.

Compliance and Certifications

Nimbus maintains a formal compliance program to support customers in regulated industries. In addition to its annual SOC 2 Type II audit, Nimbus is HIPAA-eligible for customers who sign a business associate agreement, and maintains PCI DSS Level 1 certification for handling payment-related metadata. The platform has also completed an ISO 27001 certification covering its information security management system, renewed every three years with annual surveillance audits. Customers operating in the European Union can request that their data remain exclusively in Nimbus's Frankfurt and Dublin regions to satisfy data residency requirements, and Nimbus publishes a standard data processing addendum that covers GDPR obligations for controller and processor relationships. Government and public sector customers can request FedRAMP Moderate authorization details through their account manager, though general availability of the FedRAMP-authorized environment is limited to the GovCloud region. Every compliance report and certificate is available for download from the trust center without needing to contact sales.

Multi-Region Replication and Disaster Recovery

Nimbus operates eleven regions across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, and every bucket can be configured for either single-region storage or multi-region replication. When multi-region replication is enabled, writes are acknowledged after being durably stored in the primary region and are asynchronously copied to up to two additional regions, typically completing replication within 60 seconds under normal conditions. Customers can monitor replication lag for any bucket from the dashboard's replication tab, which also exposes a Prometheus-compatible metrics endpoint for teams that want to alert on lag exceeding their own thresholds. In a full regional failover, Nimbus's control plane automatically redirects read and write traffic to the nearest healthy replica within 90 seconds, and DNS-based routing ensures client SDKs reconnect without any code changes. Nimbus tests full failover procedures on a quarterly basis in a rotating region pair and publishes a summary of each test, including measured failover time, on the public status page.

Command-Line Tooling and Developer Experience

Beyond the REST API, Nimbus ships an official command-line tool called nimbusctl, distributed as a single static binary for macOS, Linux, and Windows. nimbusctl supports recursive upload and download with automatic multipart handling for files larger than 100 MB, parallel transfer with a configurable worker count, and a sync command that mirrors a local directory to a bucket much like a traditional file-sync tool. Authentication is handled either through an API key stored in a local configuration file or through a short-lived token obtained via the sso login command for Enterprise customers using single sign-on. The tool also includes a cost-estimate command that scans a local directory and reports the projected monthly storage and egress cost before anything is uploaded, which teams commonly use during migration planning. nimbusctl is open source, and Nimbus accepts community contributions through its public GitHub repository under an Apache 2.0 license.

Webhooks and Event Notifications

Nimbus can notify external systems in real time when objects change inside a bucket. Customers configure one or more webhook endpoints per bucket, and Nimbus sends a signed HTTPS POST request whenever an object is created, overwritten, or deleted, with the request body containing the object key, size, content type, and a SHA-256 checksum of the payload for verification. Every webhook request includes a signature header computed with HMAC-SHA256 over the request body using a per-endpoint secret that customers can rotate at any time from the dashboard without any downtime. If an endpoint returns anything other than a 2xx response, Nimbus retries delivery with exponential backoff for up to 24 hours before marking the event as failed and surfacing it in the webhook delivery log. For teams that prefer a managed event bus instead of hosting their own receiver, Nimbus also supports forwarding the same event stream directly to Amazon EventBridge, Google Pub/Sub, or a generic Kafka-compatible endpoint. Event notifications are included at no extra cost on the Professional and Enterprise plans, and are available on the Starter plan for a flat fee of $5 per month per bucket.

Marketplace Integrations and Add-ons

Nimbus maintains a marketplace of first-party and partner-built add-ons that extend the core storage platform. The most popular add-on, Nimbus Vision, automatically generates thumbnails and extracts metadata such as dimensions and EXIF data for any image or video uploaded to a bucket, and costs $0.01 per processed file. Nimbus Scan runs every uploaded file through a malware and virus scanning engine before it becomes downloadable, quarantining anything flagged for manual review, and is included at no extra charge on the Enterprise plan. Teams that need full-text search across the documents stored in a bucket can enable Nimbus Search, which indexes PDF, DOCX, and plain text files and exposes a query endpoint that returns matching object keys ranked by relevance; indexing typically completes within 5 minutes of upload. All marketplace add-ons are billed on the same monthly invoice as the base storage plan, and can be enabled or disabled per bucket from the add-ons tab without requiring a support ticket. Partners who want to build and publish their own add-on can apply through the Nimbus Partner Program, which provides sandbox credentials and a revenue-sharing agreement once an add-on passes the platform's security review.
